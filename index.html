Welcome To

Raja Mahendra Pratap Singh University

As a residential-cum-affiliating State University, the institution takes pride in its rich academic heritage, distinguished alumni, and a team of experienced faculty members.

The University follows a transparent, efficient, and responsive administrative system and provides state-of-the-art infrastructure.

This includes a modern library, Wi-Fi enabled campus, and advanced research facilities, in a vibrant, secure, and student-centric environment.
