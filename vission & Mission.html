      Vission

      To emerge as a dynamic service and advisory apex organization in India by undertaking such initiatives and programmes which could strengthen and popularize Indian higher education as leading edge system in the world and promote greater national and international collaboration in Higher Education, Research and Extension, Sports, Youth and Cultural Activities.

      Mission

      To provide high quality education and research facilities to students in a conducive environment for their personality growth in the changing local, national and international scenario through advanced knowledge, wisdom and skills.

      Goal

      The University aims at creating a people-centric, sustainable, just and equanimous society through the provision of quality education, research and enriching experiences to the students that allows them to develop into well-rounded, productive and self-reliant citizens.

      Objectives

      • Imparting high quality education to youth in order to imbibe in them knowledge, skills and wisdom so as to ensure their meaningful participation in society.
      • Creating an atmosphere of receptiveness to excellence and scholarship in various disciplines.
      • Nurturing institutional efforts to creation and promotion of high-quality talent and value systems.
      • Promoting innovative and ICT based methodologies for more effective and quality-oriented learning, teaching and research.
      • Promoting entrepreneurial skills in students through modern technology.
      • Continuous engagement with alumnus, stakeholders, leaders and decision makers for meaningful contribution to the world of policies, practices and innovations.
      • Fostering collaborative partnerships among the faculty, staff and students for achieving a high-performance work environment at national and global level.
